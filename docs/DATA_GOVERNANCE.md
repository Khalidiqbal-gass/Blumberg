# Data governance gate

Before enabling an automated feed, document:

- Provider, product, exact fields, exchange/segment coverage, historical depth and update frequency.
- Free/paid tier, quotas, rate limits, downtime expectations, retention and caching limits.
- Personal research vs commercial use, display/redistribution rights, attribution and derived-data rules.
- Credential handling, renewal/cancellation terms and estimated monthly/annual cost.
- Timestamp semantics, corporate-action coverage, corrections and delisted-instrument coverage.

Do not bypass paywalls, access controls, CAPTCHAs, robots restrictions or contractual controls. Public visibility alone does not establish a right to bulk collect or redistribute data. Until a provider is approved, tests use synthetic fixtures only.
